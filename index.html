About

A little more context

I am a Senior Mobile Application Developer based in Costa Rica, working remotely. I started in native Android with Java, moved through Swift and Objective-C, and for the last several years have focused on Flutter and React Native — which is where I have delivered most of the products now live on the stores.

What I care about is the part of the job that shows up after launch: crash rates, cold start times, whether the analytics actually answer the product question, and whether the next developer can find their way around the codebase. Reducing crashes by 50% on one product was not one fix — it was testing discipline, instrumentation, and refactoring the parts everyone had been avoiding.

I like owning a product end to end. On Oeskn that means the camera and analysis flow, the subscription layer, the Firebase backend, the localization for two markets, and the release process — and being the person accountable when any of it breaks. I work in health and fintech contexts, so I treat sensitive user data as sensitive by default.

I also enjoy the team side: mentoring junior developers, keeping code review useful rather than performative, and building the shared components that stop everyone from solving the same problem five times.
